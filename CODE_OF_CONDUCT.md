# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This repository is for practical, evidence-based reliability engineering work.
Participation should make technical review safer, clearer, and more useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas, evidence, and implementation trade-offs directly and
  respectfully.
- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions and personal preference.
- Give specific, actionable feedback without attacking the contributor.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Accept correction and update claims when better evidence becomes available.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading claims, fabricated evidence, or concealed conflicts
  that materially affect a contribution.
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer has asked for a discussion to stop or
  move to a more appropriate channel.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other public interactions undertaken on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct concerns in a public thread when doing so would expose
private or harmful information. Contact the maintainer through
[jasondoyle.ie](https://jasondoyle.ie/) and identify this repository.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions
will consider the available evidence, the safety of affected people, and the
needs of the project.
